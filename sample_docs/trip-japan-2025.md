# Japan trip notes, autumn 2025

We spent 4 nights in Tokyo, then 3 nights in Kyoto, with a day trip to Nara to see the deer.

## Transport
The 7-day Japan Rail Pass cost 50,000 yen. In hindsight a few single Shinkansen tickets would have
been cheaper for this route.

## Where we stayed
In Tokyo we booked the Sakura Inn in Asakusa, a short walk from Senso-ji temple. The Kyoto
guesthouse was near Gion.

## Food
Best ramen of the trip: Menya Kaito in Shinjuku, tonkotsu with a soft egg. Go before 18:00 to
avoid the queue.
