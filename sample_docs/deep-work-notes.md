# Notes on Deep Work

Plan deep work in blocks of 4 hours at most; the quality of focus drops after that.

My rules: no phone until 11:00, and email is checked only twice a day. At the end of the working
day I run a shutdown ritual and say the phrase "schedule shutdown complete" out loud to signal to my
brain that work is over.

A weekly review happens on Friday at 16:00: what went well, what blocked me, plan for next week.
