# Garden log 2025

I planted 8 San Marzano tomato plants on 12 April. The first harvest was on 2 August and the plants
kept producing until late September.

## Care routine
Water every 2 days in summer, in the morning, at the base of the plant. Tie the stems to canes
weekly.

## Problems
Aphids appeared in June. I treated them with a neem oil spray in the evening, twice, one week
apart. Blossom-end rot on two plants was fixed by watering more regularly.
