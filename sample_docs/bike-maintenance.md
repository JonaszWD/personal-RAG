# Road bike maintenance sheet

Lubricate the chain every 200 km, and always after riding in the rain.

## Numbers to remember
- Tire pressure: 5.5 bar front, 6 bar rear.
- Full service interval: every 3000 km.
- Replace brake pads when the pad material is down to 1.5 mm.
- Stem bolts torque: 5 Nm. Carbon parts need torque-limited tools.

## Winter
Switch to wet lube and mudguards from November to March.
