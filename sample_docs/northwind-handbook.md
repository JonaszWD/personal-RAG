# Northwind Labs - Employee Handbook (fictional)

Northwind Labs was founded in 2019 in Lisbon and currently has 42 employees. The company builds
marine sensor software and is known internally for its "Friday demos".

## Time off and benefits
Every employee receives 28 days of paid vacation per year. Remote workers get a home-office
stipend of 600 euros once, in their first month. The learning budget is 1,200 euros per year and
can be spent on books, courses and one conference.

## On-call and incidents
The on-call rotation changes weekly, every Monday at 09:00. The primary on-call engineer must
acknowledge a page within 10 minutes. For security incidents, call the incident hotline on
extension 7777 before doing anything else; never delete logs.

## Equipment
All laptops use full-disk encryption and must be enrolled in device management before first use.
Lost devices are reported to IT within 24 hours.
