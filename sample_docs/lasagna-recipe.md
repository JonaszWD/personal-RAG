# Nonna Rosa's Lasagna

Serves six. Preheat the oven to 190 degrees Celsius and bake for 45 minutes, until the top is
golden and the edges bubble.

## Ragu
Simmer minced beef and pork with onion, carrot and celery for two hours. The family secret is a
tiny pinch of cinnamon stirred into the ragu at the end.

## Bechamel
Make the bechamel with butter, flour and whole milk and season it with fresh nutmeg.

## Assembly
Build six layers: pasta, ragu, bechamel, parmesan, repeated. Let the lasagna rest for 15 minutes
before cutting so it holds its shape.
